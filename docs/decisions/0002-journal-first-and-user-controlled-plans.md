# ADR 0002: Keep the diary as the source and plans user-controlled

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-10

## Context

The service is intended to turn reflection into action without making diary
writing feel like task entry.

## Decision

Journal entries remain the source record. Actions and habits may refer back to
an entry but stay independently editable, completable, archivable, and
restorable. AI may propose candidates; it cannot create plans on the user's
behalf.

## Consequences

The interface needs explicit review and add-to-plan steps. Derived plans remain
traceable to their supporting entry, while users can decline irrelevant ideas.
